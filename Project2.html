    Aerodynamic Design Project

    Project Objective

    Comprehensive
    CFD Study

    The aim was to perform a full aerodynamic study of the Drivaer fastback geometry and develop a high-performance aero package. Key components — rear wing, diffuser, and front splitter — were individually optimised to maximise downforce while minimising drag.

    The project followed a systematic approach combining steady-state CFD simulations with parametric CAD iterations. Each component was characterised individually before integration into the complete vehicle package for final balance optimisation.
